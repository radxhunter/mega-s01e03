The problems

  • parser: the shared parser read every date month first, and every tenant writes the day first. 7 April became 4 July. Every channel, every tenant.
  • hint: booking-v3’s form printed MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People typed what the hint said, and the form read it the tenant’s way, day first. Only the form channel of booking-v3 tenants. The parser fix did not touch it; retiring the form ends it.

Why intake missed it

meta.intakeTag is a guess from the reporter’s browser. The column “intake tagged otherwise” counts the reports in each problem that intake filed under the other name.

Lessons

  • A subject line says how a report feels, not what broke. Read the tenant and the channel before you merge.
  • One ticket marked known can hold problems with different fixes, and one with no fix at all.
